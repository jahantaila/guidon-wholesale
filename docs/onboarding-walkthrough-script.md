# New account walkthrough: video script

A short (about 2 minute) screen recording for new wholesale customers. It gets
linked from the "account approved" email (Settings → New Account Walkthrough).
Every step follows the current portal as of Sept 2026. Re-record it if the
login, password or checkout screens change.

**Record against a demo account, never a real customer.** Use a local or
staging copy with demo data so no real business names, prices or balances show
up. Screen size: 1280×800, browser zoom 100%.

| # | On screen | Voiceover / caption |
|---|-----------|---------------------|
| 1 | The welcome email, with the **Open wholesale portal** button highlighted | "Your Guidon wholesale account is approved. Click **Open wholesale portal** in this email." |
| 2 | guidonbrewing.com/wholesale sign-in form | "Sign in with the email the brewery has on file and the temporary password from the email." |
| 3 | The **Set your password** prompt, typing a new password (at least 6 characters) | "The first time you sign in, pick your own password. You won't see the temporary one again." |
| 4 | Portal **Overview** tab | "This is your account home. You'll see open orders, invoices and the kegs you have out." |
| 5 | **Browse & Order** tab: pick a beer, choose a size, set a quantity, add to cart | "To order, open **Browse & Order**, pick a beer and size, and add it to your cart. Sizes that are out right now are greyed out." |
| 6 | Checkout: **Review & Place Order**, filling in the empty-keg return counts, ticking the keg return checkbox | "At checkout, enter how many empty kegs you're sending back with the delivery and tick the confirmation box." |
| 7 | Click **Place Order**, then the confirmation screen | "Place the order. You'll get a confirmation email, and the brewery schedules delivery. We deliver Thursdays and Fridays." |
| 8 | **Order History**, then **Invoices** tabs | "Past orders and invoices live here. Payment is due on delivery." |
| 9 | **Account** tab | "Update your contact details, address or password under **Account**." |
| 10 | **Help** tab, then back to the email reply address | "Questions? Check **Help**, or just reply to any Guidon email. It comes straight to the brewery." |

## Before it goes in the email

1. Ask Mike whether he wants a **link** or a **file attachment**. A link is
   recommended: a 2-minute MP4 is usually 10–30 MB, too big to attach
   reliably, and large attachments hurt deliverability.
2. Host it somewhere that plays without a login (unlisted YouTube, or a
   Google Drive file shared "anyone with the link"). Open the link in a
   private window to check it plays.
3. Paste the https link into **Admin → Settings → New Account Walkthrough**.
4. Test with the **internal** approval path only: approve a test application
   that uses a Derby Digital address, never a real customer's. Check the email
   arrives, shows the walkthrough line, and the link plays.
